JD Resume Tailor

One-time setup

JD Resume Tailor runs the AI agent already on your computer (Claude Code, Codex, Grok, or DeepSeek). Connect it once: run this in Terminal.

~/jd-resume-tailor/app/install.sh

It registers a small helper with Chrome and Edge. No server, no port, no password. Then come back here.
